We integrate field measurements, inversion algorithms, and coupled process modeling to understand the complex behavior of subsurface reservoirs during fluid production and injection. Observations constrain fracture characterization, while coupled models together with inverse theory help interpret monitoring signals and predict the evolution of fluid flow, heat transport, rock/fracture deformation, and seismic response.
