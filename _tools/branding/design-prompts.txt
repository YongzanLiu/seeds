Concept A
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original, highly refined identity symbol for SEEDS Lab, an academic research group in subsurface energy, fractured reservoirs, coupled fluid/heat/geomechanical processes, and monitoring. This is a serious institutional scientific identity, ultimately used as a 16–32 pixel browser favicon. The previous literal drawing of wells and waveforms was rejected as crude. Start fresh.
Direction A: a compact, bold, beautifully proportioned abstract geological STRATA mark. Three clean, offset geological bands form one coherent circular silhouette. A single carefully shaped negative-space fault/channel cuts through them and subtly suggests the letter S for SEEDS. It must read as one designed symbol, with deliberate optical balance and generous internal negative space. Let the geological connection emerge from the geometry rather than adding pictograms.
Style: sophisticated modernist brand design, Swiss precision, flat vector-like filled shapes, sharp clean contours and thoughtfully softened structural corners. Strong and distinctive in monochrome. Two solid colors only: deep ink blue #0B293E and rich cyan/teal #109FA5. Transparent background outside the symbol. Square canvas, centered mark occupies 80% of width, balanced clear margins.
Only the standalone symbol. No lettering, labels, caption, wordmark, rounded app tile, borders, diagram, well pipe, waveform, tiny dots, decoration, shading, bevel, gradients, textures, glows or mockup. No generic eco leaf, water droplet, atom, medical ECG, or stock logo. Deliver a polished, original professional mark, not a sketch.

Concept B
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original, highly refined identity symbol for SEEDS Lab, an academic research group working on fractured subsurface reservoirs, coupled fluid flow/heat/geomechanics, numerical models and monitoring. The result is a final-quality scientific brand mark suitable for a 16–32 pixel browser favicon. The previous literal well-and-waveform sketch was rejected for looking crude. Start fresh.
Direction B: an iconic compact ISOMETRIC RESERVOIR VOLUME, reduced to a few broad, meticulously aligned filled geometric facets. A single purposeful negative-space fracture passes through the rock volume and subtly offsets two strata. Express the idea of a fractured underground medium with coupled processes through the geometry itself. The overall silhouette is a balanced, compact hexagonal/three-dimensional rock-block form, with a clean architectural sense of precision. It should feel like a refined scientific institute identity, not a database icon or a rendering.
Style: high-end modernist corporate/scientific identity, optically balanced, custom geometry, flat vector-like crisp filled shapes, no outline drawing. Only two solid colors: deep ink blue #0B293E and cyan #119DC1. Broad gaps must remain legible at tiny sizes; few shapes, a decisive silhouette. Transparent background outside the symbol. Square canvas, centered symbol occupies 80% width with equal clear margins.
One standalone mark only. No letters, text, labels, caption, wordmark, app tile, outline frame, multiple concepts, legend, gradients, 3D shading, cast shadows, bevels, decorative lines, dots, waveforms, well pipes, eco leaves, water drops, atoms, medical ECG, or stock clip art. Deliver an elegant original finished mark.
